Read first

ADE is a city condition, not a checklist.

Book one hard choice

Start with the thing you would regret missing: RAUM, a serious Westergas night, one left-field Noord party, or a listening/culture programme.

Use area logic

Westergas, Sloterdijk, Noord, RADION and the center do not combine casually after midnight. Choose a lane before you start stacking tickets.

Leave an exit

ADE gets better when you do less. Keep one night or one morning unclaimed, especially if you are booking anything that runs into daylight.

Official frame
